reCAPTCHA Enterprise — token minter

Helper for vitamix/test-recaptcha.mjs. Loads the reCAPTCHA Enterprise SDK with your site key and lets you mint a token for any of the three actions the worker recognizes. Tokens are copied to the clipboard automatically.

This page is served from an AEM delivery domain so its hostname is automatically in the tenant's CORS allow-list. No localhost setup needed.
